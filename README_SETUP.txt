GOODPICK GUIDE — FREE AFFILIATE WEBSITE STARTER
=================================================

WHAT'S INCLUDED
- index.html: homepage
- guides/: three beginner buying guides
- privacy.html and editorial-policy.html: starter drafts
- No live affiliate links, tracking pixels, analytics, forms, or external scripts are enabled.

PREVIEW LOCALLY
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, or Firefox.

PUBLISH FOR FREE (GitHub Pages)
1. Create/sign in to a GitHub account and check its age/terms requirements.
2. Create a new PUBLIC repository named goodpick-guide (or another available name).
3. Upload the contents of this folder to the repository root (index.html must be at the root).
4. Open repository Settings -> Pages.
5. Under Build and deployment, choose Deploy from a branch; select main and /(root); save.
6. Wait for GitHub Pages to publish. The site address will be shown in Settings -> Pages.
7. Test the homepage and each guide on mobile and desktop.

IMPORTANT
- Account creation, acceptance of terms, and publishing require your own authorization.
- Free hosting availability and terms may change; follow the current provider instructions.
- Do not add affiliate links until the affiliate program approves you and your site.
- Never invent affiliate tracking links or claim a product was tested if it was not.
- Check each program's country eligibility, age requirement, payout threshold, payment method, traffic rules, and disclosure rules. If you are under 18, involve a parent/guardian where required; do not misstate your age or bypass eligibility rules.
- Affiliate earnings are not guaranteed. $1,000/month is a target, not a forecast. Expect audience-building and experimentation to take time.
- Replace the sample brand if needed; check trademark/domain availability before investing in branding.
- Add a real contact method and review privacy, tax, and consumer-protection obligations applicable to your location and audience.

30-DAY ORGANIC STARTER PLAN
Week 1: Publish site; confirm links work; select one primary category (suggested: AI/productivity tools OR tech accessories).
Week 2: Publish 2 researched comparison articles and 1 how-to article, using original wording and citations to official sources.
Week 3: Share genuinely useful excerpts in communities where promotion is allowed; answer questions without spam; begin a simple content log.
Week 4: Review Search Console or other permitted analytics after configuring them transparently; improve pages that answer real questions; apply to relevant affiliate programs once the site has useful content.

CONTENT WORKFLOW
For each article: identify a specific reader question -> research official product documentation and independent sources -> record sources/date checked -> compare trade-offs -> add affiliate disclosure if monetized -> fact-check -> publish -> revisit details periodically.

INCOME MATH (ILLUSTRATIVE ONLY)
At a $10 average commission, $1,000/month requires 100 qualifying sales. If 2% of relevant visitors buy, that would mean about 5,000 qualified visits/month. Actual commissions and conversion rates can be far lower or higher; this is not a prediction.
