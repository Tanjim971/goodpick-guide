How to Choose a USB-C Hub for a Laptop

This practical checklist helps you compare options before buying. It is general guidance, not a claim that we have tested particular products.

  1. Check the laptop port. USB-C describes the connector shape, not every capability. Confirm whether your exact laptop port supports video output, data speeds, and charging.
  2. Match the display. Check the hub's supported resolution and refresh rate, and whether it can run the number of external displays you need.
  3. Check power delivery. Compare the hub's power pass-through rating with your laptop's required charger wattage. The hub may use some power itself.
  4. Choose the ports you need. Count USB-A, USB-C data, HDMI/DisplayPort, Ethernet, SD card, and audio ports. Avoid paying for ports you will never use.
  5. Check safety and returns. Look for a clear manufacturer identity, warranty, return terms, and compatibility notes. Avoid relying on a product title alone.
